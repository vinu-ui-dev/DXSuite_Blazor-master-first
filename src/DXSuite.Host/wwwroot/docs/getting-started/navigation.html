    Navigating DXSuite¶

    An orientation to the top navigation bar, which is your starting point for everything in DXSuite.

    The top navigation bar¶

    Once you are signed in, a bar runs across the top of every page. From left to right:

    1. Brand logo — Select the DXSuite logo on the far left to return to the home page at any time.
    2. Product module buttons — The main work areas (for example Director) appear as buttons next to the logo. Related pages are grouped: a grouped button opens a dropdown of its pages, while a standalone button navigates directly. The active area is highlighted.
    3. Tenant switcher — If your account belongs to more than one tenant, a switcher on the right shows the current tenant's name. See Tenants and Locations.
    4. Notification bell — Alerts and status notifications for your account.
    5. Help — Opens this documentation.
    6. Profile avatar — A circle with your initials on the far right. Select it to open your account menu.

    The profile menu¶

    Selecting your profile avatar opens a dropdown showing your name and role, followed by these items (each appears only if your role grants access):

    • Settings — Personal and workspace settings.
    • Configuration — Administrative configuration for your site.
    • Log Out — Ends your session and returns to the sign-in card.

    Result¶

    You can move between work areas from the module buttons, reach account options from the profile menu, and always return home via the brand logo.

    Why your menu may differ

    The buttons and menu items you see depend on your assigned role and the features enabled for your tenant. Two users on the same site can see different options. If something described here is missing, it is not enabled for your account.

    Tips¶

    • The highlighted module button tells you which area you are currently in.
    • Selecting anywhere outside an open dropdown closes it.

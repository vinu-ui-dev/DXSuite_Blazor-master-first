# First Login

How to sign in to DXSuite for the first time and confirm your account is ready to use.

## Prerequisites

- A DXSuite web address (URL) provided by your administrator.
- One of the following, depending on how your organization is configured:
    - A single sign-on (SSO) account — **Microsoft**, **Google**, or **Okta**.
    - A directory (Active Directory) username and password.
    - A DXSuite email-and-password account.

## Steps

1. Open your DXSuite URL in a web browser. You will land on the sign-in card.
2. Choose the sign-in method your organization uses. The card only shows the options that are enabled for your site:
    - **Sign in with Microsoft / Google / Okta** — select the provider button and complete that provider's sign-in prompt.
    - **Sign in with Active Directory** — enter your **Username or UPN** and **Password**, then select the sign-in button.
    - Email and password — enter your **Email** and **Password**, then select **Sign In**.
3. If email accounts are enabled and you do not have one yet, select **Don't have an account? Create one**, enter your **Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**, then select **Create Account**.
4. If you just registered, check your inbox for a confirmation link and confirm your address before signing in.

## Result

After signing in you land on the main DXSuite workspace with the top navigation bar visible. Your available modules and menu items reflect your assigned role and your site's enabled features.

!!! info "Verify your account and role"
    Select your profile avatar (the circle with your initials) at the top-right. The menu header shows your name and current role (for example **Admin**, **Architect**, or **Contributor**). A newly registered account may start as **New User** with limited access until an administrator assigns a role.

## Tips

- Account passwords must meet your site's complexity rules (minimum length and required character types). If registration is rejected, review the on-screen message.
- After repeated failed attempts, an account may be temporarily locked. Wait and try again, or contact your administrator.
- For security, DXSuite signs you out automatically after a period of inactivity. Simply sign in again to continue.

!!! note "Don't see the option you expected?"
    Sign-in methods are configured per site (and can vary per tenant). If a provider or the email form is missing, your administrator has not enabled it for your account.

# Tenants and Locations

Two concepts that shape everything you see in DXSuite: the **tenant** you are working in, and the **locations** that organize your displays and devices within it.

## What a tenant is

A tenant is an isolated workspace for a single organization or customer. Each tenant has its own data, users, enabled modules, and features, and one tenant cannot see another tenant's content. When you sign in, you are always working inside one tenant at a time — its name is shown in the tenant switcher on the top navigation bar.

Most users belong to a single tenant and never need to think about this. If your account has access to more than one tenant, you can switch between them.

## What a location is

A location represents a physical place or grouping within a tenant — such as a building, terminal, or site — under which the displays and the devices that drive them are organized. Locations are how you find and manage a specific set of screens, and how content and access are scoped to the right places.

## Steps: switch tenants

1. Look for the tenant name button on the right side of the top navigation bar. If it is not shown, your account has access to only one tenant and there is nothing to switch.
2. Select the tenant name to open the **Switch tenant** menu. Your current tenant is marked.
3. Select the tenant you want. DXSuite reloads into that tenant's workspace.

## Result

After switching, the navigation, modules, and data all reflect the selected tenant. You may be asked to sign in again, because each tenant maintains its own secure session.

!!! info "Modules vary by tenant"
    A tenant only shows the modules and features it is licensed for. If a work area is available in one tenant but not another, that difference is expected.

## Tips

- To create and manage locations and the displays within them, see [Locations](../infrastructure/locations.md).
- Access to specific locations can be granted per user, so you may see only the locations relevant to your work.

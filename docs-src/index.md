# DXSuite Help

Welcome to the DXSuite documentation. DXSuite is a digital signage management platform for
designing, scheduling, deploying, and monitoring content across distributed display networks.

## How this documentation is organized

- **Getting Started** — First-time login, orientation, and how tenants and locations fit together
- **Director** — Dashboards, content scheduling, overrides, approvals, and the counter scheduler
- **Designs** — Creating and cataloging display designs and their requirements
- **Locations, Displays and Devices** — The core inventory: sites, screens, and the players that drive them
- **Screenshots** — Viewing live device screenshots
- **Pulse** — At-a-glance health monitoring
- **Connectors and Integrations** — Integrating with external platforms such as Omnivex Ink
- **Configuration** — Reference data (airlines, cities) and content metadata
- **Administration** — Tenants, users and roles, audit history, and platform elevation
- **Troubleshooting** — Solutions to common problems

---

!!! info "Which version am I on?"
    Your DXSuite version is shown in the application's **Settings** area. Documentation reflects the
    current release; if a screen differs from what you see, check with your administrator about your
    deployment's version.

# Pulse

Pulse is an at-a-glance health surface that summarizes the state of your DXSuite platform on a single dashboard.

Where the [Devices](../infrastructure/devices.md) and [Screenshots](../slc/screenshots.md) views focus on individual players, Pulse gives you a high-level read on overall health so you can tell quickly whether everything is running normally.

## Prerequisites

- You must have access to the Pulse dashboard.

## Steps

1. Open the **Pulse Dashboard**.
2. Review the **Pulse Health** card. It shows an overall status indicator with a short summary of the current health state.
3. Use the dashboard as your starting point: if the summary looks healthy, no action is needed; if it does not, drill into the affected area.

## Result

You get an immediate sense of platform health from one screen, and a clear signal of when to investigate further.

!!! note
    Some monitoring detail depends on optional capabilities being enabled in your deployment. If a panel reports that a capability is unavailable, ask your administrator whether it is enabled for your environment.

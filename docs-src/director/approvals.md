# Approvals

The Approvals page is where content moves through review before it can be scheduled: reviewers act on items in their queue, and submitters track the status of what they sent.

## Prerequisites

- You must either belong to an approval group (to review content) or have submitted content for approval (to track it). Administrators can see all pending items.

## Steps

### Review items in your queue

1. Select **Approvals** in the Director navigation to open **CONTENT APPROVALS**.
2. Open the **My Queue** tab. Each row shows a preview, asset name, location, who uploaded it, your group, the current stage, and when it was submitted.
3. For an item, choose an action:
    - **Approve** — move it to the next stage, or complete approval if it is the final stage.
    - **Decline** — reject the content.
    - **Detail** — view the full stage progression and comment history.
4. In the action dialog, add notes if needed, then confirm. Your decision and any note are recorded.

### Track your submissions

1. Open the **My Submissions** tab.
2. Use the **Status** filter to show **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Declined** items.
3. For a declined item, select **Re-submit**, explain why in the required comment, and confirm to send it back into the approval flow.

## Result

Approved content becomes available to schedule on displays. Declined content is held for a limited retention period (shown as an auto-delete countdown) unless you re-submit it.

!!! info "Multi-stage approval"
    Content can pass through several stages, each involving one or more groups. The **Detail** view shows every stage, which group acted, when, and any notes. The **Stage** column shows progress as current stage over total stages.

## Tips

- Administrators also see an **All Pending** tab listing items awaiting approval across every group, for oversight.
- An item marked **AI Auto** was approved automatically by content moderation rather than by a reviewer.
- Counts on each tab show how many items need your attention.

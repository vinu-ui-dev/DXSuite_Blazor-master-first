# Dashboard

The Director dashboard is your starting point: it lists the locations you have access to, grouped for quick browsing, so you can jump straight to the site you want to work on.

## Prerequisites

- Your account must be assigned to at least one location. If none are assigned, the dashboard appears empty.

## Steps

1. Select **Dashboard** in the Director navigation. The page opens under the **LOCATIONS** heading.
2. Browse the location groups. Each group is a collapsible section with its own heading.
    - Select a group heading (or its chevron) to collapse or expand that group.
3. Locate the site you want and select its tile. Each tile shows the location image and name.
4. You are taken to that location, where you can drill into its displays and content.

## Result

You land on the selected location's page, from which you can open a display to manage its schedule and content.

!!! info "You only see your locations"
    The dashboard is filtered to the locations assigned to your account. Groups that contain none of your assigned locations are hidden. If a site you expect is missing, ask your administrator to assign it to you.

## Tips

- Groups remember their collapsed or expanded state while you stay on the page, so you can tidy the view down to the groups you use most.
- The dashboard is read-only browsing — you create and change content once you open a location and one of its displays.
